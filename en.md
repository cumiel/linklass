# Linklass Privacy Policy

**Service**: Linklass
**Operator**: Cumiel Inc.
**Last Updated**: May 11, 2026
**Effective Date**: May 11, 2026

Cumiel Inc. ("we", "us", or "operator") respects your privacy. This policy explains
what personal information we collect through the Linklass app ("Service"),
how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Korean version: [한국어](./)

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 At Sign-Up (Required)
- Email address
- Name (if provided by social login)
- Profile image URL (if provided by social login)
- Social login identifier (Google Sub ID, Apple User ID)

### 1.2 During Service Use (Optional)
- Nickname
- Bio
- Profile image (uploaded by user)
- Class information (name, description, icon image)
- Lesson schedule and bookings
- Student enrollment records

### 1.3 Automatically Collected
- FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging) push token
- Device advertising identifier (non-personally identifiable)
- App version, OS version (for diagnostics and ad serving)
- Subscription and purchase records (via Apple App Store / Google Play)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---------|-----------|
| User identification and authentication | Email, social login ID |
| Lesson booking, management, and notifications | Name, nickname, profile, FCM token |
| Sharing classes with students | Nickname, profile image |
| Subscription processing | Payment identifier, subscription state |
| Push notifications | FCM token |
| Advertising (Free/Standard plans) | Ad ID, basic device info |
| Fraud prevention and debugging | Device info, app usage logs |

---

## 3. Data Retention

We delete all personal information **promptly upon account deletion**.

| Item | Retention Period |
|------|-----------------|
| Account info (email, name, profile) | Until account deletion |
| Payment / subscription history | 5 years (Korean e-commerce law) |
| Push notification history | Auto-deleted after 30 days |
| Class / booking / credit records | Until account deletion |

Data required to be retained by law is kept for the statutory period and then deleted.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

We do **not sell or share** your personal information with third parties, except:

- When required by law or lawful request from authorities
- Payment information processed and stored by Apple/Google for in-app purchases

---

## 5. Data Processors

We share specific data with the following processors to operate the Service:

| Processor | Service | Region |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Google LLC (Firebase) | Authentication, database, storage, push notifications, serverless functions | Google Cloud (asia-northeast3, Seoul) |
| RevenueCat, Inc. | Subscription verification and management | AWS |
| Apple Inc. | iOS in-app purchases, Sign in with Apple | Apple infrastructure |
| Google LLC (Google Play / AdMob) | Android in-app purchases, Google Sign-In, ads | Google infrastructure |

Each processor handles data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

---

## 6. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

- **Access**: View your info in the in-app profile screen
- **Correction**: Edit your profile in the app
- **Deletion (account closure)**: Use the in-app "Delete Account" feature
- **Restriction of Processing**: Contact us directly (see contact below)

Upon account deletion, all personal data is removed immediately and cannot be recovered.
Data subject to legal retention obligations is stored separately and deleted after the retention period.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 14.
If we become aware that a user under 14 has created an account, we delete that
account and associated data immediately.

---

## 8. Security Measures

We protect personal data through:

- HTTPS-encrypted transport
- Firestore Security Rules for access control
- Authentication-based access (users can only see their own data)
- Firebase Auth security standards
